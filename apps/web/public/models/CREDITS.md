# 3D models

Every model here is from a pack by Kenney (https://kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0).
Each folder keeps its pack’s licence file. Copied by `node scripts/models.mjs`.

- `characters/`: Mini Characters, 13 models
- `vehicles/`: Car Kit, 6 models
- `industrial/`: City Kit (Industrial), 16 models
- `suburban/`: City Kit (Suburban), 10 models
- `commercial/`: City Kit (Commercial), 8 models
- `watercraft/`: Watercraft Pack, 4 models
- `factory/`: Factory Kit, 12 models
- `furniture/`: Furniture Kit, 9 models
- `nature/`: Nature Kit, 7 models
